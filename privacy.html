Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026 (version 1.1). Previous version: 6 September 2026.

Doors Closing collects no data whatsoever.

The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs. The game itself works entirely offline.

Purchases and ratings

The optional Full Journey purchase, Restore Purchases and the rating prompt use Apple’s own StoreKit services. Apple processes any payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your name, email address or payment details. The only thing the app keeps is a note on your device that the Full Journey is unlocked.

What is stored, and where

Your level progress, scores, stars, streaks, Daily Commute bests, rating-prompt bookkeeping and settings are saved only on your own device, using the operating system’s standard local storage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

Children

The app is rated 4+. It contains no user-generated content, no advertising and no links out to the web. The one optional in-app purchase sits behind a parental gate (a sum a grown-up has to answer), and “Rate Doors Closing” in Settings opens only the App Store.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
