Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026.

Doors Closing collects no data whatsoever.

The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests of any kind and works entirely offline.

What is stored, and where

Your level progress, scores, stars, streaks and settings are saved only on your own device, using the operating system’s standard local storage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

Children

The app is rated 4+. It contains no user-generated content, no purchases, no advertising and no links out to the web.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
