How do I move?

Drag anywhere on the play area. Your finger does not need to start on the character. Hold the SPRINT button in the bottom-right corner to run; it has a stamina meter that refills when you let go.

How do I get through a ticket barrier?

Walk up to it. A TAP BARRIER button appears; tap it and the gate opens. If your card is rejected, tap again.

My platform changed mid-run.

That is the game. Follow the cyan route line to the new platform, and check the platform label at the top left of the screen.

How do I earn three stars?

Board the train with plenty of time left, take an efficient route, and avoid bumping into other commuters.

A level is locked.

Catch the train on the previous level first. Levels 11 and 16 also need 12 and 20 total stars respectively. Levels 11 to 20 are part of the Full Journey (see below).

What is the Full Journey?

Levels 1 to 10 and the Daily Commute are free. The Full Journey is a one-time purchase that unlocks levels 11 to 20 and any level added in future updates. It is not a subscription. A grown-up check appears before the purchase screen. Family Sharing is supported.

I bought the Full Journey on another device or reinstalled.

Open Settings, then Full Journey, then Restore Purchases (also on the purchase screen). Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

What is the Daily Commute?

One level a day, picked from the date, with its own best score. Find it on the home screen. There is no streak, so missing a day costs nothing.

How do I turn off the vibration or the motion?

Settings, then Feel. You can disable haptics and enable reduced motion, which calms the camera and removes pulsing effects.

How do I replay the tutorial?

Settings, then Help, then Replay Tutorial.

Does the game need internet?

No. The game plays offline and collects no data. Only buying or restoring the Full Journey, or rating the app, talks to the App Store.

Is there sound?

Yes. Version 1.1 adds original music, station sounds and the doors-closing beeps. Music and effects have separate volume sliders in Settings, and the game follows your silent switch. If you are already playing your own music, the game keeps the sound effects and leaves your music playing.

I want to start again.

Settings, then Reset Progress. This clears stars, scores and streaks and cannot be undone. It does not remove a Full Journey purchase.
